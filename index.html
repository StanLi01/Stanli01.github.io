Stanley Osondu

Ph.D. Student, Geospatial Information Science · The University of Texas at Dallas

I am a geospatial data scientist who combines a background in surveying with machine learning to study how places change. My doctoral research at UT Dallas applies spatio-temporal and machine learning methods to disaster resilience, renewable-energy siting and fairness in geospatial AI.

I hold a B.Sc. in Surveying and Geoinformatics from the University of Nigeria and an M.Sc. in Applied Data Science (Distinction) from Teesside University. Before my Ph.D., I worked on field surveys and site mapping in Nigeria, GIS-based smart-meter and leakage analysis at Yorkshire Water, and GPS tracking analytics at Middlesbrough Football Club. I work mainly in ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Python, R and SQL.
